How to Play PUZZLE-CAM

😌📸

1. Close Both Eyes (Capture Photo)

Simply close both eyes in front of the camera. The 3-second countdown will start automatically. Open your eyes and smile!

🧩

2. 3x3 Photo Puzzle

Your captured photo is stamped with the official GDG ENSAF logo, sliced into 9 puzzle pieces, and scattered across the screen.

👆🤏

3. Drag & Drop Pieces

Use your finger to drag pieces directly on your phone screen, or pinch with one hand in the camera to move pieces into place!

💾✊

4. Save to Your Mobile

When all pieces are assembled, tap Save Photo (or hold a closed fist ✊) to save the branded photo directly into your phone gallery!
